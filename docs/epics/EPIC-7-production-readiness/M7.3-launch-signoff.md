# M7.3: Launch sign-off and known limitations

## Goal
Formalize the final launch decision by confirming the MVP is ready or by documenting any required blockers and known limitations before release.

## In scope / Out of scope
- **In scope:** launch approval checklist, blocker tracking, final sign-off, release notes
- **Out of scope:** product roadmap beyond MVP

## Tasks
1. Summarize evidence from QA and build verification.
2. Confirm or reject launch readiness based on the PRD and acceptance criteria.
3. Document known limitations or blocked items explicitly.
4. Finalize the launch checklist and owner sign-off.

## Acceptance criteria
| AC | Testable binary statement | Verification method |
|---|---|---|
| M7.3-01 | Final launch decision is recorded with evidence | Sign-off record |
| M7.3-02 | Any blocked or unverified item is explicitly listed | Release notes |
| M7.3-03 | Launch checklist is complete and reviewed | Final release review |
| M7.3-04 | The product is not declared ready without evidence | QA gate |

## Definition of Done
- Launch sign-off is complete, with evidence and explicit blockers or approvals recorded.
- Status updated in `STATUS.md`.
